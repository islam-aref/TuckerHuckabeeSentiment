# Sentiment Analysis of Pre-War-on-Iran Comments: A Study of MAGA and the American Right

I conducted a sentiment analysis of comments made a few days before the War on Iran. Before presenting my findings, I must first thank **Dr. Khalil El-Anany** for the inspiration. I wanted to quantify and scientifically test the claim that MAGA fans and the conservative right in the United States are turning against Israel's influence on American politics.

## Main Findings

The sentiment was trending toward **"America First"** — quietly predictable, knowing that MAGA supporters are "proud patriots" in one way or another.

The new finding here is that **negative sentiment toward "Israel First" was stronger than that toward America**, revealing a broadening gap between what the American right wing perceives as the nation's best interest and its most strategic ally in the Middle East.

## The Palestine Question

What was personally important for me to test was the stance on Palestine. What I found is that **Palestine is quietly not present at all in this debate**.

This challenges the narrative that those supporting cutting aid to the occupation are Arabs, lefties, commies, or pro-Palestinian activists. **They are mostly pure MAGA — and it cannot be denied.**

## Methodology

The project uses the **YouTube API from Google Cloud** to extract comments. To handle the data effectively, a representative sample of comments — varying in length, like counts, and engagement levels — was assigned scores. This dataset was then used to perform **fine-tuning on a pre-trained BERT model** to capture the nuanced sentiments and patterns within the audience discussions.
