# Sentiment Analysis of Pre-War-on-Iran Comments: A Study of MAGA and the American Right

I made a sentiment analysis for the comments on before the War on Iran by a few days, but before starting with what I got, first I have to thank **Dr. Khalil El-Anany** for inspiration, I wanted to quantify & scientifically test the claim that MAGA fans & conservative right in the United States are turning against Israel influence on American politics.

## Main Findings

The main findings were that the sentiment was going towards **"America First"**, and this is quietly predictable knowing that MAGA are "Proud patriots" in a way or another. The new finding here is that the negative sentiment on **"Israel First"** was stronger than that of America, revealing a broadening gap between what American right wing perceives as the nation's best interest and its most strategic ally in the Middle East.

## The Palestine Question

What was personally important for me to test is the stance on Palestine, what was found is that **Palestine is quietly not present at all in this debate**. So the narrative that those supporting cutting aid to occupation are Arabs, lefties, commies, or pro Palestinian activists, They are mostly **pure MAGA** and it cannot be denied.

## Methodology

The project uses the **YouTube API from Google Cloud** to extract the comments. To handle the data effectively, a representative sample of comments varying in length, like counts, and engagement levels were assigned scores. This dataset was then used to perform fine-tuning on a pre-trained **BERT model** to capture the nuanced sentiments and patterns within the audience discussions.
